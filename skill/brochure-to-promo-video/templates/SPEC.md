# SPEC: <product> promo

GOAL:    what the viewer feels or does at the end, one sentence
OUTPUT:  HyperFrames, 1080 x 1920 (delivered supersampled 2160 x 3840), 30 fps, H.264 + AAC
TIME:    narration length + a held end card; the narration is the clock. Every cue is stamped from word times.

LOOK (from the brochure; write numbers, not adjectives):
- Grounds / ink / the one accent, as hex from brand/palette.json (confirm by eye), and where the accent may appear
- Display face + weight + size in px; UI/caption face; label style (tracking, case)
- The medium: what the picture is made of (the product's own UI, real photos from the brochure, flat type on colour...)

OBJECTS: every thing the viewer must recognise, drawn so it reads without a label
MOTION:  eases by role (enter, move, exit, camera); springs as damping; durations in frames
SHOTS:   one line per shot: time range, the one hero move, what the viewer reads, the sound, the hand-off to the next shot
BANNED:  the HyperFrames defaults (dark void with floating dots, glow, particles, purple/blue gradients, three cards in a row,
         docked logo, everything fading in), plus anything this film must not become
QA:      the frames to check and what each must show
