# House rules: The Apologist Project

Apply this file when the product belongs to The Apologist Project (Apologist AI, Alim, Zogreo, ...). For any other
organization skip it and ask the user for their brand rules and who approves content.

Source: the Confluence page "Brand Quick Reference" (space Sales & Marketing, page id 366739466), as read on
2026-10-31. If the Atlassian connector is available, re-read it (it may have changed) and prefer it over this summary.

## Voice and content

- Ecumenical, not divisive; AI-positive, not fear-mongering; commentate rather than create doctrine.
- Give value before promoting; never lead with the product. Open on a person, a need or a question.
- Never position the AI as replacing pastors, missionaries, the church or Scripture. It multiplies and points toward people and Scripture.
- No fabricated statistics, user counts, accuracy claims, testimonials or beneficiaries. Quote only what the organization supplied.
- Never mock skeptics, other religions, or people who are deconstructing. Never name a competitor critically.
- Off limits: denominationally divisive content, partisan politics, original theological arguments.

## Approval

- **Tier 1, always Jake Carlson (President):** everything in a brand's first 30 days, theology, AI ethics, competitors. Treat a Zogreo promo as Tier 1 (my reading: Zogreo's accounts are new and the video quotes scripture); confirm with the user.
- Tier 3, never without Jake: crisis responses, partnership commitments, denominational statements.
- So: deliver the video as a draft for approval. Run a theological claim check on the script (does any line assert
  a doctrinal or evidential position? quote it, say what it asserts, offer a lower-risk rewrite). A lexical or
  quoted-verse line with its reference is low risk; say "no new theological claims" only if that is true.

## Brands and calls to action

- Zogreo (missions orgs and churches, B2B, funding appeals) and Apologist AI (apologist.ai, the chatbot) are separate
  brands: do not mix their numbers, logos or claims unless Jake agrees.
- Approved CTAs: B2B "Book a free consultation"; D2C "Visit apologist.ai". A donor appeal may use the brochure's own
  ask (for Zogreo: give at zogreo.org, or book a call at calendly.com/zogreo/intro).
- The brochure's own words win: reuse its phrases ("safe place to ask", "heart language", "for the long haul").

## Handling

- Deliver files to the user. Never post to social accounts or schedule posts unless asked; accounts are managed by people.
- Brand assets (logos, templates) are in the shared Drive. The Zogreo mark exists in light and dark SVG/PNG.
- Internal pages and DMs contain plaintext passwords; never copy them anywhere, and tell the user once.
- Internal finance and strategy notes are not source material for a public video. Use the published brochure and
  the figures it prints; if an internal note disagrees with the brochure, list the conflict in RESEARCH.md and
  use the brochure's figure.
