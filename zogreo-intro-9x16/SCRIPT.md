# Script

**The idea, in one sentence:** one seeker's 2 a.m. question goes unanswered because the harvest is plentiful and the workers are few, until Zogreo answers it in her language and a local believer joins; the next morning everyone has an answer, and a gift makes that possible.

## Voiceover (ready to record; also in `SCRIPT-FOR-RECORDING.md`)

It's two in the morning. Layla saw a ministry's ad, and asks a question they've never had a safe place to ask.

But messages pour in faster than a small team can answer.

So seekers wait. Or the ministry turns off its ad just to catch up, and the door closes.

The harvest is plentiful, but the workers are few.

That's why we built Zogreo. It's the word Jesus used for catching people alive.

Zogreo answers seekers day or night, in their heart language, and it's honest about the hard parts.

And when someone is ready for a real person, it brings in a trained believer from a local ministry.

In a weeklong pilot in Turkey, Zogreo helped a two-person team serve 3.73 times as many seekers.

Zogreo doesn't replace missionaries. It multiplies them.

Your gift puts that conversation within reach of someone who has never had a safe place to ask.

Give today at zogreo.org.
