## 3. Synthesized (my choices, not facts)

- The story order: the responder's problem, the pilot, the result, the hostile messages, the ask.
- The narration's own wording around the quotations (L2 to L9, L11, L12). L11 "Our AI doesn't replace the team. It multiplies them." follows the brand voice ("AI multiplies people and never replaces them"); it is not on the page.
- **Pictures are illustrations, not data:**
  - The message bubbles across the map (no counts are published).
  - The path that narrows after each study (the page calls the funnel "intentionally rigorous"; no drop-off figures are published).
  - The 100 dots against 373 dots (a picture of the 3.73× ratio).
  - The two equal bars (a picture of "approximately the same").
  - The jagged bubbles and the line around the team (a picture of the AI absorbing hostile messages).
- "Followed up on obedience" on the study cards shortens the page's "follows up to see if they went through with their acts of obedience".
- End-card tagline "Accelerate the Great Commission" (the one-pager's donor header, as in the intro film).

- Tier 1: the whole cut goes to Jake (Troy while he is away), including naming Kavanah Media and Waha on screen.
