# Storylines

Source: the Zogreo donor one-pager ("Zogreo — Missions.pdf"). The film gets the same points across in the same order a donor needs them: the need, the mechanism, the proof, the ask. Per the brand voice rules it never leads with the product.

## A. The 2 a.m. message (recommended, built)

Device: a moment in someone's day; one object (a seeker's first message) travels through the whole film. Form: object-to-object chain.

A ministry's phone lights up at 2:07 a.m. with "Hi, I saw your ad. I'm curious: what is the hope you talked about?" (the org's own example conversation, from the Zogreo conference slide). The message sinks under newer ones and the ad gets paused. Then the camera pulls back: it is one bubble in a field of thousands, "the harvest is plentiful". The field gathers into the word zōgreō. Back on the phone, the AI answers in Arabic in seconds, works through the hard part honestly, marks Layla ready, and a believer from a local ministry joins the same thread. The two-person team's 3.73× lands, and the film ends on the give card.

- First 2 s: dark forest-green night, the phone at 2:07 AM, Layla's message already on screen (the thumbnail).
- Beats: hook (message arrives) → tension (it waits, the ad is paused) → verse (one bubble among thousands) → reveal (zōgreō, "to catch alive") → mechanism (AI answers in her language; ready; handoff to a believer) → proof (3.73×) → claim (multiplies, not replaces) → end card (give, book a call).
- Look: the flyer's own system. Forest green #1E3A21 and cream #F6F3EC, Cormorant Garamond Light with italic accents, Inter for UI and captions, the phone UI in brand colours.
- Sound: VoiceStudio narration as the clock; a notification on each message arrival, a soft click on the ad toggle, whooshes on the two big camera moves, a riser into 3.73×. No music bed (no music source in this session).

Why this one: a donor can picture one real seeker and one small team, and it shows the same "AI responds first, a human disciples next" mechanism the new zogreo.org explainer uses.

## B. Ripe fields (not built)

Device: type carries the argument. Form: one frame that rebuilds. John 4:35 arrives word by word over a cream field. Each word sprouts conversations like stalks until "ripe" fills the frame. Two worker marks cannot reach them all; Zogreo threads the field and routes the ready stalks to the two marks, and the 3.73× is printed as the field's yield. Beautiful, but the seekers become abstract marks, and an explainer that turns its people into shapes loses the donor.

## C. Two responders (not built)

Device: life before and after. Form: split screen to one. Two responders in Türkiye work side by side at night, their queues overflowing (before). The panes collapse into one calm screen where the AI holds the overflow and hands them only the ready conversations (after), and the 3.73× lands. Strong proof, but it centres the staff's workload instead of the seeker, and it shows a real pilot team we have no footage of.
